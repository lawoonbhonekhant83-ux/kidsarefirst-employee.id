The article chronicles William's imaginary rise to public prominence, his legendary facial expressions, and the increasingly dramatic attention surrounding his supposed journey.

Background [edit]

William first came to public attention after his friends began collecting photographs that were considered far too powerful to remain in a private camera roll. The collection later inspired the creation of this unofficial encyclopedia entry.

According to completely fictional sources, the announcement of this page caused a significant increase in group-chat activity.

Biography [edit]

William is described by his friends as a memorable individual with a distinctive sense of humour and an unusually large collection of photographic evidence. His biography remains under development by the unofficial editorial team.
